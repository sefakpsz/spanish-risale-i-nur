# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Readers using a focused mobile book library. They browse multiple books, read one chapter or page at a time, save progress and bookmarks, and may use English or Spanish for the interface while Spanish is the release content language.

## Product Purpose

Provide a calm, accessible reader foundation for authorized Spanish editions of the Risale-i Nur collection. The active development catalog contains only *Tratados Sobre Ramadán, Frugalidad y Agradecimiento* while publisher permission is unresolved.

## Brand Personality

Calm, literary, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid a generic social feed, ad-driven bookstore, gamified reading dashboard, or store-like purchase funnel. Avoid presenting development fixtures as published editions or implying guaranteed store approval.

## Design Principles

- Reading remains the primary action on every reader screen.
- Content provenance is clear and honest during development.
- Library and reader patterns are reusable for multiple books.
- Language choices remain explicit and easy to change.
- Interactions support careful reading without distracting motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable type, strong contrast, centered text as requested, clear labels, touch targets suitable for mobile, selectable text where possible, and reduced-motion-friendly interactions. Arabic annotations need a non-color cue through press feedback and an accessible label.
